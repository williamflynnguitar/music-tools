Composition Assignments

These are exercises I’ve used for years and give to students studying composition with me one-on-one. Many of the tunes I’ve recorded and released started in these exercises.

Nothing is more frustrating than wanting to write a tune and staring at a blank page because you think inspiration has to strike before you can put down a chord or a note. Exercises like these get the gears turning. Usually, somewhere in the middle of one, your instincts take over and you start following the tune that’s forming in your head. That’s always the goal, so give yourself permission to abandon the rules of the exercise if you reach that moment.

And if no tune shows up and instinct never takes over, finish the exercise as written anyway. You’ll have something complete to come back to later and retool.
