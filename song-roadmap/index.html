Glossary

the gold number
Every section has a number. In rehearsal, "from 7" means start at section 7.
bar
One measure. Each box in the chord grid is one bar. A heavier line marks every four bars.
%
Repeat the chord from the bar before.
Eb7 × 16 bars
Stay on that chord for 16 bars.
N.C.
No chord. The band lays out or plays without harmony (drums alone, a unison line).
·
A dot holds the previous chord for one more beat. "Bb7 · · B7" is three beats of Bb7, then B7 on beat 4.
‹Eb7
A push. The chord arrives an eighth note early, ahead of the beat.
Eb7!
A stop or hit. Play the chord short, then silence.
open / repeat until cued
The section keeps repeating until someone gives the signal to move on. "32 max" means it won't go longer than that.
→ (the then-line)
What happens at the end of the section: who comes in, what the signal is, where the band goes next.
as 4 Verse 1
Play the same chords as that section. "Up a half step" means the same chords one fret higher.
vamp
A short pattern repeated over and over, usually open.
tag
The last line or phrase repeated, usually to set up the ending.
pickup
Notes that come just before the downbeat of a new section.
MD
Music director. The person who gives cues on stage.
count-in / start cue
How the song begins: who counts, or what you listen for.
segue
Go straight into the next song without stopping.
B♭ / E♭ instruments
Trumpet, tenor sax and clarinet read B♭ parts. Alto and bari sax read E♭. Pick yours in stand view and the chords change to what you read.
tier
How ready the band keeps a song: concert set (polished), gig book (playable on request), pocket (can pull it out in a pinch).

Text syntax

The Build tab writes this text for you. The Text tab is for pasting a draft or editing by hand.

Header lines come first, one field: value per line. Fields: title artist band key tempo time length start ref lead backing tier status.

# Chorus | 8
@ 0:48
lyric: first few words
Bb7 | B7 | Bb7 . . B7 | Ab7 | <Eb7 | Eb7 x2 | Eb7!
-> horn stabs bars 7-8; back to riff
note: Bass: walk up in bar 8

# starts a section. After | comes the bar count: 8, open, or 16 open 32.

Chord row: one bar per |. Eb7 x16 writes sixteen bars. A lone chord fills the section. Inside a bar, . holds for a beat, < before a chord is a push, ! after it is a stop.

= 4 uses the chords of section 4. = 4 +1 uses them a half step up.

-> is the then-line. Numbers after "to", "into" or "from" become jump links. @ 0:48 is where the section starts on the recording. lyric: is a few words to find your place by. note: adds a note for players; start it with Bass: or Horns: to tag a part. Any other line is a description (desc: forces that).

Stand view keys: ↓ j space next section, ↑ k previous, a number then Enter to jump, ← → previous and next song in a set, Esc to close.
