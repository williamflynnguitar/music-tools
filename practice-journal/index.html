      Where this lives

      Everything you log here is stored in this browser, on this device, and nowhere else. There's no account and no server — which is why it opens instantly in a practice room with no wifi, and why nothing you write here is visible to me or to anyone else. The trade is real, so here it is plainly: clearing your browsing data, using private browsing, or switching to another browser or another phone means these entries are gone, and I can't get them back for you. Export a backup now and then. The JSON file is the whole journal and it imports straight back in — once a month, or any time before you clear something out, is plenty.
