Music tools

William Flynn

Small practice tools for jazz guitar, built for my students. Each one is a single page that works offline once loaded — open it in the practice room, phone in hand.

  • 2 & 4two-and-four/ A time trainer that clicks on beats 2 and 4, then fades out bar by bar so your inner clock does the work.
  • Fretboardfretboard/ The movable scale fingerings from the handbook — ten scales, every key, drawn in the book's orientation.
  • Scales deckscales-deck/ Scale Practice 101 as a deck of cards: pick a key, a concept and a tier, and step through the exercises.
  • Arpeggios deckarpeggios-deck/ Arpeggio practice three ways — parallel shapes, the horizontal and the vertical diatonic approach.
  • Chartwrightchartwright/ A chord-chart editor with self-adjusting spacing — lyrics with the changes placed right over the syllables. Charts save on your device.
Companion to the Jazz Guitar Technique Handbook, 3rd edition; the fingering data is derived from it.
