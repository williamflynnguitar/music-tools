# Triads over a bass note — worksheet for William

The "Over a bass note" tab ships the **major** table from Ex. 21 (*Introduction
to Jazz Guitar*, p. 27). Practice suggestion 1 on the same page tells the
student to repeat the process for minor, augmented and diminished triads, and
the book prints no tables for them. Those 36 cells show "not yet written" in
the app until you rule them here.

**How to read a row.** "Triad on" is the interval of the triad's root above
the bass note, the way p. 27 says to think about it. "Tones" names each triad
tone against the bass. "Proposal" is Claude's reading, worked out from the
tones and nothing else. It is a starting point for you to correct, and none of
it is in the app. A dash means no sensible 7th-chord reading was found. Two
readings separated by · mean the cell would carry both, as Ex. 21's B♭ cell
does.

**How to answer.** Write your symbol in the last column, X being the bass
note. Write a dash for a cell you want left empty, and "ok" to accept the
proposal as it stands. Entering them is a data edit: each quality is one
array of twelve cells in `TBN`, in `index.html`.

The 𝄫7 of the brief's draft is written °7 here, under the no-double-accidentals
rule (2026-09-12).
