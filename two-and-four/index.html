Round

Slower is harder here — the gap between clicks widens and it's easier to flip. The wood-block count-in orients you on 1; the round starts on the bar after it. Tap only beat 1, the silent beat.

Rungs

Every rung keeps the click on 2 & 4; each one withdraws some downbeat evidence. You are marked Ready after one stretch of 8 straight bars with every tap on the downbeat, within 60 ms on average. Ready marks last for this page visit only — nothing is stored.

Dropout

Phrase: clicking bars are spread evenly through each phrase rather than bunched at the front, and thin out toward your density floor. A click always lands on bar 1 of the phrase.
